How it works

You set offers. The lake economy clears.

1 · Choose a goalLake health, local prosperity, or a blend. The frontier is recomputed for that goal.
2 · Set offersOffer tokens shift your willingness to supply capital. They are not direct final capital.
3 · Run economyOther investors and firm demand respond through a hidden response matrix.
4 · Learn the systemCompare offers with actual capital added, outcomes, and position relative to the frontier.
